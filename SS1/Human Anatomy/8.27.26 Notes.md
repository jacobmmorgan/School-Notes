**RUQ**  
Liver, gallbladder, right kidney, and portions of the stomach, small intestine, and large intestine

**LUQ**  
Left lobe of liver, stomach, spleen, pancreas, left kidney, and portions of the small and large intestines

**RLQ**  
Appendix, cecum, and portions of the small and large intestines; right ovary and fallopian tube in females

**LLQ**  
Descending and sigmoid colon and portions of the small intestine; left ovary and fallopian tube in females

Gastric = belly
