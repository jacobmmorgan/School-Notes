The glycocalyx acts as both **slippery** and **sticky** depending on its location, structure
Changes to slippery when dealing with blood vessels & capillaries
Changes to sticky due to: - **Survival (Staying Put):** Bacteria use the stickiness to anchor themselves to surfaces
- **Protection (Strength in Numbers):** Sticky sugar layers allow individual bacteria to glue themselves together. This creates a tough protective shield called a **biofilm** (like the plaque on your teeth) that blocks out antibiotics and the immune system.
- **Communication (Catching Signals):** In the human body, cells use the sticky outer layer like Velcro to catch passing chemical signals, nutrients, or immune cells that need to deliver important messages.
